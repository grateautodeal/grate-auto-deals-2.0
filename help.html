Clear Answers & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to our help center! We know that working with an auto broker for the first time brings up important questions. Below, you will find clear, straightforward answers about how we partner with you to find your next vehicle.

# About Our Services And Process

1. What is an auto broker, and how are you different from a traditional dealership?

We are not the direct sellers of the vehicles; rather, we act as your dedicated liaison between dealerships and you, the client. While traditional dealerships try to sell you whatever is sitting on their lot, we work entirely for you. We handle everything upfront, navigating the market, negotiating terms, and managing the entire purchasing process, to ensure you get the exact vehicle you want without the typical dealership hassle.

2. Do you have physical cars on hand to look at?

We do not keep physical inventory on hand. Instead, we take a hands on approach throughout the entire process to ensure the vehicle checks all of your boxes. Once we help locate and source the right vehicle from our dealer network, test drives are always welcome and can be arranged for you!

3. Can you find a specific make, model, or color?

Yes, absolutely! Since we source vehicles specifically for our clients, you can let us know your exact preferences, and we will do the heavy lifting to track down the right match for you.

# Pricing, Financing And Trade Ins

4. How does financing work if I buy through a broker?

We offer different options regarding lenders, and we will assist and walk you through the choices that best fit your needs and what you are looking to accomplish. Whether you prefer to secure financing through your own institution or through OEM special financing, we guide you through the process step by step.

5. Can I trade in my current vehicle?

Yes, you can trade in your vehicle! We utilize third party appraisal techniques to help secure the best possible value for your trade in, based on the current market and the condition of the vehicle.

# Inspections, Warranties And Vehicle History

6. Do the vehicles come with a vehicle history report?

Yes! We offer vehicle history reports for pre owned vehicles through the dealership (such as CARFAX or AutoCheck) so you can review the vehicle's history in detail. (Note: Vehicle history reports are provided upon customer request, so just let us know if you would like one pulled for your vehicle of interest.)

7. Are inspections and warranties included?

We follow through with a mechanical inspection upon request to ensure your peace of mind. Additionally, options like extended warranties or service protection plans are subjective to customer requests, and we are happy to tailor these options based on your personal preferences and needs.

Still Have a Question?

We are here to manage every detail and give you complete peace of mind. If you have any other questions or concerns, please reach out via our general inquiry form on the home page.
