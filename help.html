# Brokerage Standards & Operational Framework

1. What is an auto broker, and how do your services differ from a traditional dealership?

Grate Auto Deals operates strictly as an independent buyer's advocate rather than a retail dealership. Traditional dealerships are financially incentivized to liquidate inventory currently sitting on their lots. In contrast, our fiduciary responsibility is solely to you. We navigate the market independently, negotiate strict pricing parameters, and manage the procurement process to protect your capital and eliminate dealership pressure tactics.

2. Do you maintain physical vehicle inventory for on-site inspection?

We do not maintain retail parking lots or physical inventory. Instead, we execute targeted nationwide sourcing campaigns based on your exact criteria. Once a matching vehicle is secured within our dealer network, formal appointments and test drives are coordinated directly with the holding facility under our structured oversight.

3. Can you source specific configurations, trims, or colorways?

Yes. Our acquisition service is custom-tailored to your exact specifications. We scan wholesale and retail networks nationwide to locate, verify, and secure exact-match vehicles that meet strict market valuation thresholds.

# Financial Structuring & Trade-In Protocols

4. How is transaction financing structured during a brokered purchase?

We provide structured financial guidance to align with your capital allocation strategy. Whether you intend to leverage external banking institutions, credit unions, or manufacturer promotional financing programs, we review all terms to ensure absolute compliance and prevent hidden finance charges.

5. How are trade-in valuations assessed?

We employ rigorous market data analytics and independent appraisal methodologies to evaluate your current vehicle. Our objective is to secure maximum trade-in value by neutralizing dealership undervaluation practices using verified historical transaction data.

# Verification, Inspection & Protection

6. Are comprehensive vehicle history reports provided?

Yes. Official historical data reports (such as CARFAX or AutoCheck) are pulled and audited for every pre-owned candidate vehicle. These documents are provided directly to the client to ensure total transparency regarding previous ownership, service records, and title status.

7. What mechanical inspections and protection warranties are included?

Independent mechanical inspections are integrated upon request to verify component integrity. Extended service contracts and mechanical breakdown protections are offered as customizable addendums, structured precisely to match your long-term ownership requirements.
