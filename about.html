Why I Started Grate Auto Deals

Having spent nearly a decade immersed in the automotive industry—ranging from high-volume sales at Honda Culver City[cite: 1] to leading finance operations as a Senior Finance Manager for groups like AutoNation Volkswagen Mall of GA[cite: 1] and Asbury Automotive Group[cite: 1]—I’ve seen every side of the car-buying business from the inside out. I hold a Bachelor of Arts in Accounting and Information Systems from Grambling State University[cite: 1], giving me a rigorous, analytical foundation in financial structure, credit assessment[cite: 1], and regulatory compliance[cite: 1].

Yet, even with all that background knowledge, whenever I stepped into the consumer shoes, I felt the same frustrations everyone else does: the endless waiting, the exhausting sales tactics, inventory shortages, and the constant anxiety of winding up in a bad contract. I watched good people lose hours of their weekends, get bogged down by bad business practices, or struggle with a lack of transparency simply because dealerships are built to maximize their own margins, not yours.

That is why I launched Grate Auto Deals.

I wanted to change the narrative. As a consumer myself, I know how valuable your time and peace of mind are. I've personally experienced the sting of losing customers in my past corporate roles simply because dealerships were bogged down by red tape, slow processes, or lack of inventory. I realized that my insider knowledge—structuring deals, analyzing complex credit profiles[cite: 1], and negotiating directly with a vast network of lenders[cite: 1]—belonged on your side of the table.

💡 The Customer's Perspective: What It Feels Like

"Imagine waking up on a Saturday morning dreading the thought of spending 6 hours at a dealership, arguing over hidden fees, dealing with high-pressure finance pitches, or worrying if your credit score is going to be used against you. Now, imagine skipping all of that entirely—relaxing at home while an industry insider negotiates the best terms, protects you from predatory fine print, and delivers your car straight to your driveway. That is the peace of mind I want every client to experience."

Whether you have pristine credit, are working to rebuild bad credit, have no credit at all, or are ready to pay cash, my goal is simple: to bring you the absolute best deal possible without you having to lift a finger. You don't have to spend your Saturday sitting under fluorescent dealership lights or fighting off high-pressure sales reps. I handle the negotiating, the contract auditing, the lender navigation, and the legwork from start to finish.

"My promise is transparency, safety, and a genuinely honest conversation. I'm here to make the process effortless so you can drive home knowing you got an incredible deal you can trust."
