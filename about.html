Why I Started Grate Auto Deals

I spent nearly ten years working inside dealerships. I ran high-volume sales floors and managed finance departments for luxury and volume brands like BMW, Volkswagen, Infiniti, Honda, and Toyota.

That background gave me a clear, unvarnished look at how deals are structured, how lenders evaluate risk, and where showrooms build profit into a transaction.

Even with all that inside knowledge, stepping onto a traditional showroom floor as a retail buyer still felt exhausting. I watched regular people lose entire weekends to waiting games, high-pressure sales tactics, and confusing contract terms.

Dealerships are built to protect their bottom line. They are not built to protect your time or your wallet.

That reality is why I launched Grate Auto Deals.

I wanted to flip the script. I knew that my background in deal structuring, credit evaluation, and wholesale lender networks belonged on your side of the table.

💡 The Customer Perspective: What It Feels Like

"Imagine waking up on a Saturday morning dreading the thought of spending six hours at a dealership, arguing over hidden fees, dealing with high-pressure finance pitches, or worrying if your credit score is going to be used against you. Now, imagine skipping all of that entirely, relaxing at home while an industry insider negotiates the best terms, protects you from predatory fine print, and delivers your car straight to your driveway. That is the peace of mind I want every client to experience."

Whether your credit is pristine, you are working on rebuilding it, or you are paying cash, my goal is straightforward: secure the best vehicle and financing terms for you without you ever stepping foot in a dealership.

I handle the phone calls, price negotiations, contract reviews, and delivery logistics from start to finish so you do not have to lift a finger.

"My promise is absolute transparency and straightforward communication. I handle the heavy lifting so you drive home knowing you got a deal you can trust."
